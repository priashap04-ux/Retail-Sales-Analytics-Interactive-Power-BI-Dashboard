Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

Overview  
Multi-category retail transactional data analysis across regions, cities, customer segments.

Key Insights
- NYC leads performance; Philadelphia/Houston/Chicago show sales but negative profits  
- Technology drives sales; Office Supplies has broader geographic spread  
- Discounts significantly hurt profitability  
- West region excels with strong margins  

Technical Implementatiomn  
- Data cleaning and relationship modeling  
- 12+ DAX measures (Profit Margin%, Discount Efficiency, City Profitability Rate)  
- 5-page interactive dashboard: Executive Summary, Regional Analysis, Product Performance, Customer Insights, Discount Impact  

Technologies
Power BI • DAX • Data Modeling • Business Intelligence
